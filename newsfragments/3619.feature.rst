`MemorySendChannel.set_max_buffer_size` and `MemoryReceiveChannel.set_max_buffer_size` now allow changing a memory channel's buffer capacity at runtime, without rebuilding the channel. All clones share the new setting: shrinking keeps already-accepted values (blocked senders complete once enough room opens up), growing immediately wakes waiting senders in FIFO order, and a capacity of ``0`` retains the rendezvous semantics.
