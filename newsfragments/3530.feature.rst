`MemorySendChannel` now has a `~trio.MemorySendChannel.close_with_error`
method for terminating a memory channel when a producer fails. Any send
clone may call it; the first failure closes every clone and wins. Buffered
values are still delivered, and receivers then get the new
`~trio.ChannelClosedWithError` exception (a subclass of `~trio.EndOfChannel`)
that carries the original reason in its ``reason`` attribute and
``__cause__`` chain. Tasks blocked in ``send`` are woken immediately with
`~trio.BrokenResourceError`. Plain ``close`` keeps its existing
reference-counted semantics, and closing the receive side first continues to
take priority.
